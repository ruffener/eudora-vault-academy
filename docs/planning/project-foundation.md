# Eudora Vault Academy Website — Project Foundation v1

## Client

Eudora Vault Academy

## Known details

- Indoor pole vault facility
- Coaching from former collegiate vaulters and veteran coaches
- Location: 39425 W 111th St, Eudora, KS 66025
- Email: eudoravaultacademy@gmail.com
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/eudoravaultacademy/
- Domain: eudoravaultacademy.com

## Phase 1 goal

Create a clean, mobile-friendly website that establishes credibility, explains training options, builds parent trust, and makes it easy for athletes/parents to contact or register.

## Primary audiences

- Parents of new or developing vaulters
- Middle school and high school athletes
- Club/open athletes
- School coaches looking for training options

## Primary calls to action

- Email EVA
- View training options
- Ask about availability
- Register or request information

## Recommended Phase 1 site sections

1. Hero / quick value proposition
2. Training options
3. Facility and safety information
4. Coach credibility
5. Contact and location
6. FAQ, once content is available
